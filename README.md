# ugc-brand-loyalty-analysis
A data-driven research project analyzing the impact of user-generated content (UGC) and online reviews on brand loyalty using SPSS and statistical techniques.

📌Project Overview

This project analyzes the impact of User-Generated Content (UGC) and online reviews on brand loyalty in the online fashion retail industry.

The study is based on primary data collected from 100 respondents and applies statistical techniques to uncover how digital influence shapes consumer behavior.

🎯 Objectives

Analyze how online reviews influence purchase decisions

Examine the role of UGC (photos, videos, ratings) in building trust

Evaluate the impact of positive and negative reviews on brand perception

Study the relationship between digital influence and brand loyalty

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

SPSS

Microsoft Excel

Data Analysis Techniques (Descriptive Analysis, ANOVA, Reliability Test)

📂 Dataset

Primary data collected through structured questionnaire

Sample Size: 100 respondents

Includes demographic data, review behavior, and brand perception metrics

📈 Key Analysis Performed

Descriptive Analysis (consumer behavior trends)

Reliability Analysis (Cronbach’s Alpha = 0.854)

One-Way ANOVA to test hypothesis

📊 Key Results

Online review influence significantly impacts brand loyalty (F = 7.214, p < 0.05)

UGC awareness has a strong effect on brand loyalty (F = 10.797, p < 0.05)

High reliability of dataset (Cronbach’s Alpha = 0.854)

Positive reviews increase trust and repeat purchases, while negative reviews reduce brand preference

💡 Insights

Consumers heavily rely on peer-generated content over advertisements

Digital trust plays a crucial role in shaping purchase decisions

Managing online reviews is critical for brand success
